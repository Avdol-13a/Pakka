Pakka helps small farm hosts in Pakistan make a clear promise before a visit. First, the host saves what the farm actually offers: opening hours, group size, visit duration, meals, transport, and activities. Next, paste an English guest request. Pakka compares each detail with those saved facts, and shows the original sentence. Too many guests, unavailable transport, or an unclear arrival time stay visible. The host can correct every interpretation before approving. The receipt then lists inclusions, exclusions, and open questions in English and Urdu. Guest agreement is still pending. Once cached, the app works offline, even after reload. Its small local classifier is experimental; explicit rules and host review decide the promise.
